Preparation runs in the foreground; training supervisors detach. Campaign paths
reuse batch two to detect rather than overwrite previously started experiments:
  results/e1/E1_BATCH2_CVRP50_S3009_SERVER/
Progress: scheduler_SERVER/status.json and supervisor.log; runs/METHOD/train.log;
native workers additionally write native_status.json/native_train.jsonl.
If any assigned method has formal artifacts or a preflight, the shell reports
existing runs and does not start another. A failed/interrupted run needs explicit
resume with its frozen config/checkpoint; this wrapper is not an automatic resume
or retry tool. Other not-yet-started methods in a partial campaign can be launched
individually through run_e1.sh launch --method METHOD after their smoke check.
Do not set E1_CVRP50_CAMPAIGN to hide an existing run accidentally.
E1_BATCH2_ROOT, E1_BATCH2_ASSETS_ROOT, E1_DATA_ROOT and E1_PYTHON are portable overrides.

Test after training on an idle GPU (one command per method; batch8):
  bash scripts/run_e1.sh evaluate --campaign CAMPAIGN --method METHOD --checkpoint BEST --device cuda:0 --batch-size 8
Controlled BEST is under CAMPAIGN/artifacts/checkpoints/Cus_50_CS_0/RUN_NAME/seed_3009/checkpoint_best.pt.
Native BEST is CAMPAIGN/runs/METHOD/native_checkpoints/best.pt.
Result/acceptance: CAMPAIGN/runs/METHOD/test/instances.jsonl and manifest.json.
