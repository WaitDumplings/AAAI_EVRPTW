E1 shared decoder distance row: historical audit and scope

At original commit f388343, DriverQueryEncoder has no independent distance row.
DynamicGraphKVEncoder._candidate_features reads the directed current-to-candidate,
candidate-to-depot and added-depot-detour distances (original decoder.py 532-540),
places them at slots 12, 13, 14 (632-634) in its 30-feature vector, and projects
them into AGDA action keys and bias. Its disabled forward returns zero (667-668).
No independent base-distance-row deletion was found: disabling all road encoder
paths and AGDA leaves the original base unable to observe candidate road distances.

E1 explicitly splits a shared distance-only branch from this candidate pathway.
It preserves those slots and original clamps, LayerNorm(30), Linear/SiLU forms,
action-key and scalar-bias insertion points, tanh scales and zero output heads.
Other slots are zeros; there is no resource input or hand-written negative-distance
prior. The row uses the already normalized physical directed road matrix without
additional normalization or Euclidean substitution. Initial outputs are zero;
the heads learn distance sensitivity from policy training.

model.e1_base_distance_row defaults to False. It is enabled identically for the
five controlled E1 methods. This is an explicit E1 shared-input protocol completion,
not a claim that the oldest model already had an independent base row. All old
recipes and checkpoints keep their previous outputs and signatures when disabled.
